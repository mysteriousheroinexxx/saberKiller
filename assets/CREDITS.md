# Asset Credits & Licenses — Saber Kill Simulator

Provenance and licensing for every non-original-code asset in `assets/` and `vendor/`.
The game is offline-capable; world textures, audio, and most props are generated
procedurally at runtime and carry no external license.

## Vendored libraries (`vendor/`)
- **three.js r147** (`three.min.js`), **GLTFLoader.js**, **SkeletonUtils.js**
  - License: MIT (three.js authors, https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js).
  - Vendored locally so the game runs offline; r147 is the last global-loader release
    (required for cloned SkinnedMesh locomotion).

## Procedural / original (no external license)
- **All wall/floor/ceiling/prop textures** (yellow wallpaper, damp carpet, ceiling tiles,
  concrete, stone, crate, etc.) — generated in-game by the original KTG canvas noise-operator
  pipeline in `index.html`. Approach inspired by kkrieger's `.kkrieger` / werkzeug procedural
  texture idea; **no werkzeug code copied** — the generator here is original.
- **All audio** (ambient drone / fluorescent hum, whispers, weapon/combat SFX) — synthesized
  at runtime via WebAudio in `index.html`. Original.
- **`prop_barrel.glb`** — original low-poly model generated by `tools/blender_prop.py`
  (Blender 5.3). Original work.
- **NPC portraits** — generated procedurally on canvas (`makePortrait` in `index.html`). Original.
- **Level layouts** — original procedural generators (`layoutBackrooms/Warehouse/Pipes/Dungeon`).

## User-provided assets (supplied by the project owner for this game)
- **`saber.glb`** — character model supplied by the user. Rigging + animation clips
  (`saberWalking/Falling/JumpKickAndPunch/Punches`, and their `*_clean.glb` Blender-normalized
  exports) were produced via **Mixamo (Adobe)**. Mixamo assets are royalty-free for use in
  interactive projects per the Mixamo General Terms of Use (https://www.mixamo.com/faq);
  they are **not CC0** and may not be redistributed as a standalone asset pack.
  `*_clean.glb` are derivative re-exports (unit-normalized) of the user's Mixamo downloads.
- **`handgun.glb`, `shotgun.glb`** — weapon viewmodels supplied by the user.
- **`makimaSorcererIdleStanding_clean.glb`** and **`makimaSorcererActions.glb`** —
  normalized derivatives of the user's `makimaSorcererIdleStanding.glb` and
  `makimaSorcererRunning.glb` Mixamo exports. The standing model supplies the wizard
  mesh and idle clip; the compact animation-only file supplies idle/run clips on the
  same rig. Blender 5.3 normalization removes the stray Icosphere and fixes the
  Armature's 0.01 scale. **`makimaSorcerer_baseColor.png`** is extracted from the user's
  `makimaTpose.glb` and reattached to the animated mesh, whose eight chunks share the
  original material/UV layout. Same Mixamo usage terms as the Saber clips.
- **`blood_decals_psx.glb`** — “Blood Decals PSX” by raydevv, sourced from
  [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/blood-decals-psx-d036a3b7b3d24e2d84b06905f3d301fe),
  licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Used for
  Summoner and Saber death decals; enemy hit bursts remain procedural.
- **`pfp.png`** — HUD character portrait, an anime illustration supplied by the user
  (license unspecified by the source; used here at the owner's direction).

  textures from *Budget Backrooms* scrapped folder (`hl2/vending`), adapted for in-game Backrooms flavor.
  Kept for local project use only; not for standalone commercial redistribution.

## Notes on Milestone 8 (online CC0 resources)
- 8.1 (CC0 textures) and 8.2 (CC0 props/NPC meshes) were **evaluated and deemed unnecessary**:
  the procedural texture pipeline and the Blender prop pipeline produce satisfactory results
  across all five levels (verified by screenshot), so no ambientCG/PolyHaven/Kenney downloads
  were required. Should procedural quality fall short later, CC0 sources (ambientCG,
  PolyHaven, Kenney, Quaternius) are the intended fallback and would be recorded here.
